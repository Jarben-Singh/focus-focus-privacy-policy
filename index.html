Privacy Policy

Focus Focus · Last updated: October 9, 2026

In short: Focus Focus does not require an account, does not collect or receive any personal data, and has no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking. Everything you create stays on your device. The only network activity is the app checking for updates with Expo's update servers (details below).

1. Information we collect

We do not collect or receive any personal information. The app has no sign-up or login, no backend server operated by us, and we do not receive your tasks, habits, classes, or any other content you enter.

2. Local storage on your device

The content you create is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. This includes:

  • Tasks, projects, and recurring tasks;
  • Habits and their completion history;
  • Subjects, class schedules, and exams;
  • Routine settings (sleep and meal times);
  • Streak, gems, challenges, and statistics;
  • Your theme preference and the date the app was first opened.

This data is not sent to us or to any third party by the app. Deleting the app removes this data from your device. Depending on your device settings, the operating system may include app data in your own device or cloud backups, which are under your control.

We do not store any data on servers, so there is nothing for us to delete: uninstalling the app removes all of your data from your device. Notifications can be turned off at any time in iOS Settings.

3. Notifications

The app asks for permission to show notifications. They are local notifications scheduled on your device: a reminder shortly before a scheduled task, and alerts about overdue or unscheduled tasks. We do not use push notification services and do not receive a push token.

The app uses the operating system's background fetch mode to periodically check your locally stored tasks and schedule these local notifications. This process makes no network requests.

4. Home screen widget

To display your upcoming schedule, the app shares a small amount of information with its own widget: the names and times of today's and tomorrow's items, your current streak, and your theme. This information is stored in an App Group shared only between the app and its widget on your device, and it does not leave your device.

5. Updates and network activity

The app uses Expo's over-the-air update service (EAS Update) to receive bug fixes and improvements. When the app checks for an update, your device connects to Expo's servers. As with any internet connection, these servers receive your IP address and technical information needed to deliver the right update, such as the platform, the app and runtime version, and the identifier of the currently installed update.

These requests never include your content (tasks, habits, classes, or any other data you create in the app). Expo's handling of this technical data is described in Expo's privacy policy.

6. No third parties

The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social-media SDKs, and it does not share data with advertisers or data brokers. The only third-party service involved is Expo's update service described above.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It does not ask for names, email addresses, or any account details.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the updated version at this address and revise the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to mindspot.contact@gmail.com.

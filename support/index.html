Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. The app does not require an account or sign-up.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. It is not sent to any server.

How do I delete my data?
Uninstalling the app deletes all of your data.

How do I turn off notifications?
Go to iOS Settings > Notifications > Focus Focus.

Notifications aren't showing up.
Make sure notifications are turned on for Focus Focus in iOS Settings, and that a Focus mode isn't blocking them.
